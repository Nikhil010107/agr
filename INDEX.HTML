        09 · Sources

        How this archive is built

        • Every statistic comes from an official publication: the Livestock Census, the Economic Survey of Maharashtra, or ICAR-NBAGR breed registers.
        • Every historical entry is tagged as archaeology, classical text, historical record, policy or science.
        • Dates of ancient texts are scholarly estimates. Where scholars disagree, the range is shown.
        • Practices are marked as supported by research, under study, or heritage only. Heritage remedies are not medical or veterinary advice.
        • Community profiles describe traditional livelihoods, not fixed identities, and should be reviewed with the communities themselves.

        Ethics and consent

        • Traditional knowledge belongs to the communities who hold it. Contributors must have the knowledge holder's permission.
        • Sacred or restricted knowledge is not recorded without explicit consent.
        • Knowledge holders are credited by name if they wish, and the project supports benefit sharing under the Biological Diversity Act, 2002.
        • Anyone can ask for an entry to be corrected or removed.
